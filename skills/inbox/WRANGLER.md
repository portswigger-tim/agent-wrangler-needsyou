When you cannot proceed correctly without the human deciding, approving or doing something, or you are about to end your turn by asking them to choose (next steps, "should I proceed?", a "Decisions for you" list), call `request_human_input` so it lands in their "Needs you" queue, then end your turn: the answer arrives as a message starting `[Needs you]`. Never use it for progress, summaries or "done" notices. If another session launched you, the tool refuses: ask that session with `send_message` instead. See the `inbox` skill before using it.
